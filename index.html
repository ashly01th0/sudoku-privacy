Privacy Policy for Sudoku

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · App: Sudoku for Android (com.ashlythampi.sudoku)

The short version

Sudoku does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no internet access.

What the app stores, and where

To let you continue a game and see your statistics, the app keeps the following only on your device:

  • the puzzle you are currently playing, your entries, notes, hints used, mistakes and time;
  • for each difficulty level, the number of games you have won and your best time;
  • your settings (theme, text size and whether mistakes are checked).

This information never leaves your device through the app. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android's own backup on your phone (for example "Backup by Google One"), Android may include the app's saved game and statistics in that backup, so they can be restored to a new phone. This backup is controlled by you and your phone's settings, is handled by Android, and is not accessible to the app's developer.

Links

The About screen has buttons that open this privacy policy and an open-source licence in your phone's web browser. The app itself does not go online; your browser opens the page, under your browser's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or by clearing its storage (Settings → Apps → Sudoku → Storage → Clear storage).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
